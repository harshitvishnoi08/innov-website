Master
Planning

We bring strategic clarity to complex sites — translating brief, context, and vision into coherent spatial frameworks that guide every subsequent design decision. Our master planning process begins with rigorous site analysis: topography, climate, circulation, views, and the broader urban or natural context.

Whether planning a resort campus, an institutional complex, or a large residential development, we establish the ordering principles that make the whole greater than the sum of its parts — optimising land use while preserving the character that makes each site unique.

  • Site Analysis & Feasibility Studies
  • Land Use & Zoning Strategy
  • Circulation & Access Planning
  • Landscape & Open Space Framework
  • Phasing & Development Strategy
  • Resort & Hospitality Campus Planning
  • Institutional Complex Planning
  • Mixed-Use Development Planning
  • Infrastructure & Services Coordination
  • 3D Masterplan Visualisations

Urban
Planning

We design communities — from township-scale residential developments to urban regeneration schemes that breathe new life into existing fabric. Our urban planning practice integrates land use, mobility, ecology, and public space to create places where people genuinely want to live, work, and gather.

Every urban plan we produce is grounded in local context: the climate, the culture, the existing settlement pattern, and the aspirations of the people who will call it home. The result is a plan that is both technically rigorous and deeply humane.

  • Township & Residential Layout Planning
  • Mobility & Street Network Design
  • Public Space & Parks Planning
  • Mixed-Use & TOD Planning
  • Urban Regeneration & Infill Strategies
  • Ecological & Green Infrastructure Planning
  • Regulatory & Zoning Compliance
  • Density & FSI Optimisation
  • Community Facilities Programming
  • GIS Mapping & Site Data Analysis
