Project Overview

A Landmark Riverfront Resort & Banquet Architecture in Prayagraj

Sangam Pooja Wellness Resort is a luxury riverfront wellness resort in Prayagraj, envisioned as a destination where spirituality, celebration, and refined hospitality converge. Set along the sacred banks of the Ganga, the 2.5-acre master plan is carefully oriented to maximise uninterrupted river views while preserving openness and serenity.

Designed with a royal Indian architectural influence reinterpreted through a contemporary lens, the project blends majestic character with modern functionality. More than a hospitality development, it is conceived as a landmark riverfront estate where banquet spaces, resort villas, landscaped lawns, and wellness environments integrate seamlessly into a harmonious architectural experience.

Expansive river-facing banquet spaces and landscaped lawns create an elegant setting for weddings and large-scale celebrations. Integrated resort villas, wellness zones, and leisure landscapes offer a harmonious retreat where spirituality and luxury coexist.

The Architecture in Detail

Design Intent

Where Spirituality & Luxury Coexist

Blending royal-inspired architecture with contemporary hospitality design, the master plan maximises uninterrupted river views while preserving the sacred character of the Ganga banks.

The 2.5-acre site is organised as a layered riverfront estate — the main palace anchoring the arrival sequence, with resort villas stepping down toward the water's edge. The public ghats, designed in sandstone with carved chhatris, extend the resort's presence to the Ganga, creating a ceremonial connection between the built form and the river.

Expansive banquet spaces open onto manicured lawns oriented toward the river, making them an exceptional setting for weddings and large-scale celebrations. The landscape strategy weaves together formal gardens, flowering borders, palm avenues, and water features — creating a sense of depth and layered beauty across the estate.
