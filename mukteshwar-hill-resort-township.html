Project Overview

A Ridge Chosen for Its
360° View

This concept master plan sits on an 11-acre ridge site in Mukteshwar, chosen specifically for its uninterrupted 360° views across the surrounding Kumaon hills. Rather than a single building, the brief called for a small township — a community of 45 private villas, a cluster of serviced studio apartments positioned as staffed, lock-and-leave secondary homes, and a resort property providing the shared hospitality core that ties the whole community together.

The master plan follows the same discipline we bring to any resort or hillside site: read the land first. Villas are sited along the contour to protect privacy and preserve view corridors for every unit, the studio-apartment cluster is grouped for efficient shared servicing, and the resort clubhouse anchors the highest, most view-commanding point of the ridge — with dining, lounge and wellness spaces designed to serve villa owners and resort guests alike.

Materially, the concept draws on the stone-and-timber vernacular of the Kumaon hills — pitched roofs, deep verandahs, and heavy stone plinths that anchor each structure into the slope — reinterpreted with the glazing and spatial generosity of a contemporary hill resort.

Master Plan

Three Typologies,
One Community

01
45 Private Villas
The township core — individually sited along the ridge contour so every villa gets its own framed view and privacy from its neighbours, connected by a network of pedestrian paths and stepped stone terracing.
02
Serviced Studio Residences
A grouped cluster of studio apartments positioned as staffed, 24-hour serviced secondary homes — a lower-maintenance, lock-and-leave alternative to a standalone villa within the same community.
03
Resort Property
The shared hospitality core — arrival, dining, lounge, wellness and event spaces anchored at the ridge's best vantage point, serving both resident villa owners and resort guests.
