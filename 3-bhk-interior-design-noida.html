3 BHK Interior Design Cost in Noida
A Full Room-by-Room Breakup (2026)

Ar. Udit Vishnoi 8 June 2026 12 min read

"What will it cost to do up my 3 BHK?" is the question every new flat owner in Noida asks — and the answers online swing wildly from ₹5 lakh to ₹30 lakh. So here is a straight one: what a 3 BHK interior actually costs in Noida in 2026, broken down room by room, with what sits behind every number and where you can genuinely save.

Interior cost is driven by three things — your carpet area, the quality of materials and finishes, and how much custom work you want. A standard Noida 3 BHK runs about 1,200 to 1,600 sq ft, and that is the size all the figures below are based on. Adjust up or down for your own flat.

3 BHK Interior Design Cost in Noida (2026)

Here is the headline answer first — total turnkey interior cost for a standard 3 BHK in Noida, by quality level:

Quality Level Total Cost (3 BHK) Approx. / Sq Ft
Basic / Essential₹7–10 lakh₹600–900
Mid-Range₹10–18 lakh₹1,000–1,500
Premium₹18–25 lakh₹1,500–2,200
Luxury₹25–35 lakh+₹2,200–3,000+

Most end-use families in Noida land in the mid-range to premium band — roughly ₹12–20 lakh for a complete, well-finished 3 BHK. Below ₹8 lakh you are doing essentials only (kitchen, wardrobes, basic ceiling and paint); above ₹25 lakh you are paying for bespoke joinery, imported materials and automation. These are interior figures — separate from the construction cost of building a house in Noida, which is quoted differently. Room-by-Room Cost Breakup

The single most useful way to understand — and control — your budget is to see where the money actually goes. Here is a typical mid-range breakup for a 3 BHK in Noida:

Item / Room Typical Cost
Modular kitchen₹1.5–4 lakh
Wardrobes (3 bedrooms)₹2.5–5 lakh
False ceiling & lighting₹1.5–3 lakh
Living & dining (TV unit, sofa, furniture)₹2–4 lakh
Painting & wall treatments₹1–2 lakh
Bedroom furniture & beds₹1.5–3 lakh
Soft furnishings, curtains & décor₹0.75–1.5 lakh

The two biggest line items are almost always the kitchen and the wardrobes — together they often account for nearly half of a 3 BHK budget. That is exactly why they are where good planning pays off most.

The Kitchen — Your Biggest Single Cost

A modular kitchen in a Noida 3 BHK runs ₹1.5–4 lakh, and the finish matters more than anything else. As a guide to shutter finishes:

  • Laminate — the most economical, durable and the right choice for most families.
  • Acrylic — a high-gloss, premium look at a higher price.
  • PU (polyurethane) — the most expensive, with a flawless painted finish.

Beyond shutters, the cost is driven by the layout (L-shaped is most common in Noida flats, U-shaped and parallel cost more), tall units, the carcass material, and hardware brands. One Noida-specific tip: the area's hard water makes good-quality sinks, taps and waterproof carcasses a genuine long-term saving, not a luxury. What's Included in a 3 BHK Package

"Interior design package" means different things to different firms, so the most important thing you can do is confirm the scope in writing. A standard turnkey 3 BHK package should cover:

  • Modular kitchen with base and wall units.
  • Wardrobes and storage in all bedrooms — sliding or hinged.
  • False ceilings and lighting — ambient, task and accent.
  • Wall treatments — paint, texture and feature wallpaper.
  • Core furniture — sofa, beds, dining and the TV unit.
  • Soft furnishings — curtains, rugs and styling.

Premium packages add bespoke joinery, smart home control, automation and personalised luxury furniture. If you are still deciding who to trust with the project, our guide on how to choose the best interior designer in Noida covers the questions and red flags that matter most.

Modular vs Turnkey — Which to Choose

One early decision shapes both your budget and your experience: a modular package, or a full turnkey project.

  • Modular — factory-made kitchen and wardrobe units fitted on site. Faster, easier to budget, and ideal if your civil work and ceilings are already done.
  • Turnkey — the complete home: civil work, false ceilings, painting, electrical, furniture and styling, delivered ready to move in. Costs more, but you get a fully coordinated result and one accountable point of contact.

For a new, bare flat where you want a cohesive, finished home with minimal stress, turnkey almost always wins. For a quick refresh of an already-liveable flat, modular can be enough. The same coordination logic applies to smaller apartment interiors in Noida too.

Timeline — How Long It Takes

A full 3 BHK interior typically takes 55 to 90 days from design sign-off to handover. Factory-made modular elements speed things up; bespoke joinery and imported materials extend it. The single biggest cause of delay is not the build — it is late decisions. Lock the design, finishes and fittings early, place long-lead orders first, and the schedule holds.

"The most expensive interior is the one you redo. Spend the time getting the design and the material choices right on paper — it costs nothing, and it saves you the lakhs that go into breaking and rebuilding."

How to Save Without Cutting Corners

Controlling a 3 BHK budget is about spending where it shows and saving where it doesn't:

  • Spend on the kitchen and wardrobes — you use them daily for years; this is not where to economise.
  • Economise on the easily-changed — wallpaper, décor and soft furnishings can be upgraded later cheaply.
  • Choose laminate over acrylic/PU where it won't be noticed — the durability is similar.
  • Don't over-build false ceilings — a simple peripheral design often looks cleaner and costs far less than a full ceiling.
  • Finalise everything before work starts — changes mid-project are the single biggest source of cost creep.
  • Get an itemised quote — a clear line-by-line BOQ, not a lump sum, is your best protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the interior design cost for a 3 BHK in Noida in 2026?

A 3 BHK interior in Noida typically costs ₹7–10 lakh for a basic fit-out, ₹10–18 lakh for mid-range, and ₹20–35 lakh or more for premium and luxury, on a turnkey basis for a standard 1,200–1,600 sq ft flat. That is roughly ₹800 per sq ft at the basic level and ₹1,800–2,500+ for premium.

What is included in a 3 BHK interior design package?

A standard package includes the modular kitchen, wardrobes and storage, false ceilings and lighting, wall treatments such as paint and wallpaper, core furniture like the sofa and beds, and soft furnishings such as curtains and rugs. Premium packages add bespoke joinery, smart control, automation and luxury furniture. Always confirm the exact scope in writing.

How much does the modular kitchen cost in a 3 BHK in Noida?

The modular kitchen is usually the biggest line item, costing about ₹1.5–4 lakh depending on layout and finish. Laminate shutters are the most economical, acrylic and PU cost more, and the layout (L-shaped, U-shaped or parallel) plus appliances and tall units drive the final figure.

How long does it take to design and finish a 3 BHK in Noida?

A full 3 BHK interior typically takes 55 to 90 days from design sign-off to handover, depending on scope and material lead times. Modular elements speed things up; bespoke joinery and imported materials extend it. Locking the design early and ordering long-lead items first is the surest way to stay on schedule.

What is the difference between modular and turnkey interiors?

A modular package covers factory-made kitchen and wardrobe units fitted on site — faster and easier to budget. A turnkey project is the complete home — civil work, ceilings, painting, electrical, furniture and styling — delivered ready to move in. Turnkey costs more but gives a fully coordinated result and a single point of accountability.

Budget to a Plan, Not a Guess

The honest summary: budget around ₹12–20 lakh for a well-finished mid-to-premium 3 BHK interior in Noida in 2026, put the bulk of it into the kitchen and wardrobes, finalise every choice before work begins, and insist on an itemised quote. Treat any number well below ₹8 lakh with caution until you have seen exactly what it covers.
