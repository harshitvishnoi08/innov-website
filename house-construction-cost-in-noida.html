House Construction Cost in Noida
Per Sq Ft Rates & Full Breakdown (2026)

Ar. Udit Vishnoi 6 June 2026 12 min read

"What will it cost to build my house?" is the first question every plot owner in Noida asks — and the hardest to get a straight answer to. So here is one, in plain numbers: what construction actually costs per square foot in Noida in 2026, where every rupee goes, and what makes the figure swing.

Construction cost is quoted on a per square foot of built-up area basis. In Noida and Greater Noida in 2026, that figure sits anywhere between roughly ₹1,800 and ₹5,000 per sq ft depending on the quality of construction — which is a wide range, so the rest of this guide breaks down exactly what moves you up or down within it.

All the figures below are realistic 2026 market estimates for a turnkey build (materials + labour + basic finishes). They exclude the cost of land. Use them to sanity-check quotes and plan a budget — then get a proper estimate for your specific plot and design.

House Construction Cost per Sq Ft in Noida (2026)

The single biggest driver of your per-square-foot rate is the quality of construction — the grade of cement, steel, tiles, fittings, doors and finishes you choose. Here is how the 2026 rates break down by quality level in Noida and Greater Noida:

Quality Level Cost / Sq Ft What You Get
Economy / Basic₹1,800–2,200Standard bricks, basic vitrified tiles, essential fittings
Standard₹2,200–3,000Branded fittings, good tiles, solid doors, quality paint
Premium₹3,000–3,800Designer finishes, modular kitchen, quality joinery, false ceilings
Luxury₹4,000–5,000+Imported marble, home automation, bespoke interiors

Most independent homes built for end-use in Noida land in the standard to premium band — between roughly ₹2,500 and ₹3,500 per sq ft. Below ₹2,000 you are firmly in basic-finish territory; above ₹4,000 you are paying for genuinely luxury materials and detailing.

Total Cost by House Size

To turn the per-sq-ft rate into a real budget, multiply it by your total built-up area (the sum of all floors). Here is what a turnkey build typically costs across common sizes in Noida, at three quality levels:

Remember that on a typical plot you build across multiple floors, so a 200 sq yd (≈1,800 sq ft) plot with a ground-plus-two structure can easily reach 4,000–5,000 sq ft of built-up area — and the budget scales with that total, not the plot size.

Where the Money Goes — Cost Breakup

Understanding how a construction budget is split helps you spot where a quote is padded and where it is genuinely worth spending. As a rough guide, here is how the cost of a standard Noida home divides across the major work heads:

Work Head Approx. Share
Foundation & RCC structure25–30%
Brickwork & plaster12–15%
Flooring & tiling10–12%
Doors, windows & joinery10–12%
Electrical & plumbing (MEP)12–15%
Painting & finishes7–9%
Kitchen, fixtures & fittings8–10%

Two useful rules of thumb: the structure (foundation + RCC + masonry) is around 35–40% of the whole build, and labour runs about 25–30% of total cost, spread across all of the heads above. Cement and steel alone — the two prices everyone watches — typically make up 15–20% of the total.

Grey Structure vs Turnkey — Know What You're Quoting

The most common confusion in construction quotes is the difference between grey structure and turnkey cost. They can differ by nearly half, so never compare two rates without checking which is which.

  • Grey structure (core & shell) — foundation, RCC frame, brickwork, plaster, and basic plumbing and electrical conduiting. No flooring, paint, doors, kitchen or fittings. This is roughly 50–55% of the total, or about ₹1,000–1,500 per sq ft.
  • Turnkey (ready-to-move-in) — everything: the grey structure plus flooring, painting, doors, windows, bathrooms, kitchen and all finishes. This is the full ₹1,800–5,000 per sq ft figure from the table above.

If a contractor's rate looks suspiciously low, it is almost always a grey-structure number. Always confirm the scope in writing before you compare.

What Makes Your Cost Go Up or Down

Two homes of the same size in the same Noida sector can differ 50% in cost. These are the factors that decide where you land:

  • Finish quality — by far the biggest lever, as the tables above show. The structure barely changes; the finishes change everything.
  • Number of floors — more floors spread the foundation cost but add structure, staircases and a lift, which can raise the per-foot rate.
  • Soil & foundation — soft or filled soil in parts of NCR may need piling, adding roughly ₹200–400 per sq ft.
  • Design complexity — curved walls, cantilevers, large column-free spans and double-height spaces can add 15–25% over a simple rectangular plan.
  • Locality within Noida — material and labour logistics vary between the older sectors, Greater Noida West, and the YEIDA / Expressway belt.
  • Material inflation — cement and steel prices drift up 6–10% a year, so a build that starts a year later costs meaningfully more.

Costs People Forget to Budget

The per-sq-ft rate is only the construction cost. A realistic project budget also needs to account for the items that sit outside that headline number — the ones that catch first-time builders off guard:

  • Architectural & structural design fees — typically 5–10% of construction cost, and the spend that controls everything else.
  • Government approvals — map sanction, completion certificate and related authority charges.
  • Boundary wall, gate & site development — rarely included in the built-up rate.
  • Premium interiors — bespoke modular kitchen, wardrobes, and designer interiors over and above standard finishes.
  • Landscaping, water tank, and external services.

This is also where a good architect earns the fee back. Engaging an architect early prevents the expensive mid-construction changes — walls broken and rebuilt, services rerouted — that routinely add lakhs to a project. How to Control Cost Without Cutting Corners

Controlling a build budget is not about choosing the cheapest everything — it is about spending where it matters and saving where it doesn't:

  • Design efficiently — a compact, well-planned layout with minimal wasted circulation costs less to build and run. This is where good architecture and interior design pays for itself.
  • Spend on the structure, economise on the visible-and-replaceable — never compromise on steel, cement, waterproofing or plumbing buried in the walls; you can always upgrade tiles or paint later.
  • Fix the design before you start — finalise the plan, the finishes and the fittings before construction begins, so you avoid costly changes mid-build.
  • Get the orientation and Vastu right early — a well-oriented, naturally lit home costs less to run for decades; see our Vastu tips for home construction.
  • Get a detailed BOQ — a proper bill of quantities, not a vague per-foot promise, is your best protection against cost creep.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the house construction cost per sq ft in Noida in 2026?

Turnkey construction in Noida and Greater Noida costs roughly ₹1,800–2,200 per sq ft for economy quality, ₹2,200–3,000 for standard, ₹3,000–3,800 for premium, and ₹4,000–5,000+ per sq ft for luxury. These are built-up area rates covering materials, labour and basic finishes, and exclude land, boundary wall and bespoke interiors.

How much does it cost to build a 1000 sq ft house in Noida?

A 1,000 sq ft house costs approximately ₹20 lakh at economy quality, ₹24–26 lakh at standard quality, and ₹34 lakh or more at premium quality, on a turnkey basis. The figure rises with the number of floors, the finish level, and any piling needed for soft soil.

What is the difference between grey structure and turnkey cost?

Grey structure covers only the foundation, RCC frame, brickwork, plaster and basic conduiting — about 50–55% of the total. Turnkey is the complete, ready-to-move-in house including flooring, painting, doors, windows, kitchen and fittings. Always confirm which one a quoted rate refers to before comparing.

Does the construction rate include architect fees and interiors?

Usually not. Per sq ft construction rates cover civil work and basic finishes. Design fees, premium or bespoke interiors, modular kitchen and wardrobes, landscaping, boundary wall and approval charges are typically over and above the headline rate, so budget them separately.

What factors increase construction cost in Noida?

The biggest factors are finish quality, the number of floors, soil condition (soft soil may need piling, adding ₹200–400 per sq ft), design complexity such as curved walls or large spans (which can add 15–25%), the locality within Noida, and material inflation, which raises costs roughly 6–10% a year.

Build to a Plan, Not Just a Rate

The honest summary: budget around ₹2,500–3,500 per sq ft for a good-quality independent home in Noida in 2026, keep 10–15% aside for the costs that sit outside the construction rate, and treat any number well below ₹2,000 with suspicion until you have seen the scope. The per-foot rate is a starting point — a detailed design and BOQ are what turn it into a budget you can trust.
