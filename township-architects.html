Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of township projects do you design?
Villa townships, gated communities and hill townships. Our township portfolio includes a 15-acre villa township master plan in New Chandigarh, organised around a central lake with a resort-style clubhouse, and an 11-acre hill township in Mukteshwar with 45 private villas, serviced studio residences and a resort clubhouse. Both are currently in the concept design phase.
How much land do I need for a township?
Most Indian states define an integrated township as a project above a minimum size, commonly in the 25–100 acre range depending on the state's township policy. Smaller gated communities of 5–15 acres are also planned as mini-townships, without qualifying for state township incentives. The right size depends on your state's policy and what you want to build.
What approvals does a township need, and how long do they take?
A township typically needs change of land use (CLU), a layout plan sanctioned by the local development authority, RERA registration before any sale or booking, environmental clearance once the built-up area crosses the EIA threshold, and NOCs for fire, water and electricity. Master planning and the full approval sequence typically take 6–12 months, depending on the state and the site. We prepare the master plan and layouts with these submissions in mind from day one.
What does township development cost per acre?
Industry estimates put basic township infrastructure (roads, drainage, water and power networks, common areas) at roughly ₹30–32 lakh per acre. This covers land development only, not building construction, and varies with terrain, soil, state infrastructure norms and how much land goes to roads and utilities versus sellable plots.
Do you design only the master plan, or the clubhouse and villas too?
All of it. One studio handles the master plan, the clubhouse and amenity buildings, villa architecture and interior design, from concept through construction-ready drawings. You don't need to coordinate between a planner, an architect and an interior firm.
Can you plan a township anywhere in India?
Yes. Our township work spans Punjab and Uttarakhand, and we travel to new sites anywhere in India for the right project. Every township starts with a visit to read the land: topography, access, views and the state's township policy.
