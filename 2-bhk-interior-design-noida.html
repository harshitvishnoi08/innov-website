2 BHK Interior Design Cost in Noida
A Full Room-by-Room Breakup (2026)

Ar. Udit Vishnoi 13 June 2026 11 min read

The 2 BHK is the most common home in Noida — and the one with the widest range of interior quotes, because "doing up a 2 BHK" can mean anything from a clean ₹6 lakh fit-out to a ₹20 lakh designer home. This is a clear, honest breakdown of what 2 BHK interior design actually costs in Noida in 2026: the turnkey budgets by quality level, and a full room-by-room breakup of where the money goes.

The cost of a 2 BHK interior in Noida depends on three things: the quality of materials and finishes you choose, how much custom joinery (kitchen, wardrobes, panelling) is involved, and the size of the flat. The figures below are realistic 2026 market rates for Noida and Greater Noida flats — use them to set a budget, then get a proper estimate for your actual home.

2 BHK Interior Cost in Noida by Quality Level

On a turnkey basis — design plus full execution for a standard 700–1,100 sq ft flat — here is what a 2 BHK interior costs in Noida in 2026, by the quality of the package:

Quality Level Total Cost (2 BHK) Approx. / Sq Ft
Basic / Essential₹6–9 lakh₹1,000–1,400
Mid-Range₹9–15 lakh₹1,500–2,000
Premium₹15–22 lakh₹2,200–3,000+

Most end-use families in Noida land in the mid-range band — roughly ₹9–15 lakh for a complete, well-finished 2 BHK. Below ₹6 lakh you are doing essentials only (kitchen, wardrobes, basic ceiling and paint); above ₹15 lakh you are paying for bespoke joinery, imported materials and richer finishes. The kitchen and the two bedroom wardrobes are the biggest items — together they often take 40–50% of a 2 BHK budget, because they are custom-built joinery with hardware. What a 2 BHK Interior Package Includes

A turnkey 2 BHK package — design plus execution under one roof — typically covers:

  • Modular kitchen — base and wall units, countertop, and provision for chimney, hob and sink.
  • Wardrobes in both bedrooms, with internal organisers and loft storage.
  • TV / entertainment unit and living-room storage.
  • False ceilings and lighting in the key rooms, with recessed and cove lighting.
  • Painting and any feature-wall finishes (panelling, wallpaper, texture).
  • Loose and fixed furniture — beds, dining, seating, as scoped.
  • Design service — consultancy, layouts and 2D/3D visualisations.

What is often quoted separately: flooring, bathroom renovation, and major civil work like moving walls or plumbing. When you compare two quotes, the first question is always the same — what exactly is included?

What You Get at Each Budget Level

The same 2 BHK can be finished for ₹7 lakh or ₹18 lakh. The difference is entirely in the materials and the amount of custom work:

Basic (₹6–9 lakh)

Laminate-finish modular kitchen and wardrobes, false ceilings limited to the living and dining, standard emulsion paint, and a mix of value furniture. A genuinely clean, liveable home — ideal for a rental unit or a first home.

Mid-range (₹9–15 lakh)

Custom joinery with soft-close hardware, membrane or acrylic kitchen shutters, a quartz or granite counter, more panelling and cove lighting, a feature wall or two, and semi-custom furniture. This is the sweet spot for most Noida families — polished and durable without luxury price tags.

Premium (₹15–22 lakh+)

Acrylic and PU finishes, fuller false ceilings with designer lighting, veneer and stone cladding, statement lighting, and bespoke furniture throughout. Every surface is considered — a compact home that feels genuinely luxurious.

How to Get the Most From Your 2 BHK Budget

In a 2 BHK, where space is tight, spending wisely matters more than spending less. The proven ways to stretch the budget:

  • Prioritise the kitchen and wardrobes — the items you use every day earn the best return; economise on the second bedroom and rarely-used corners.
  • Mix finishes intelligently — acrylic on the kitchen face, laminate inside the cabinets; nobody sees the difference, your wallet does.
  • Use full-height storage — in a compact flat, going up to the ceiling adds storage without eating floor space.
  • Keep false ceilings to the living and dining — flat ceilings with good lighting work perfectly in the bedrooms.
  • Blend custom and ready-made furniture — built-ins where you need the fit, store-bought sofas and beds where you don't.
  • Lock your scope before you start — mid-project additions are where compact-home budgets quietly overrun.

Above all, a 2 BHK rewards good space planning more than any other home — the right layout makes a small flat live large. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 2 BHK interior design cost in Noida in 2026?

A 2 BHK interior in Noida typically costs ₹6–9 lakh for a basic fit-out, ₹9–15 lakh for mid-range, and ₹15–22 lakh or more for premium and luxury, on a turnkey basis for a standard 700–1,100 sq ft flat. That is roughly ₹1,000–1,400 per sq ft at the basic level and ₹2,500+ for premium.

What does a 2 BHK interior package include?

A turnkey package usually covers the modular kitchen, wardrobes in both bedrooms, a TV and entertainment unit, false ceilings and lighting, painting, and loose and fixed furniture, plus design consultancy and 2D/3D drawings. Flooring and major civil work are sometimes quoted separately, so always confirm the scope.

Where does most of the money go in a 2 BHK interior?

The modular kitchen and the two bedroom wardrobes are the biggest items, together usually taking 40–50% of the budget because they are custom-built joinery. The living room (TV unit, seating, false ceiling) is next, followed by lighting, painting and soft furnishings.

How long does a 2 BHK interior take in Noida?

A turnkey 2 BHK interior typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from design sign-off to handover, depending on the amount of custom joinery and any civil work. Factory-made modular kitchens and wardrobes speed this up; on-site carpentry and false ceilings add time.

How can I reduce my 2 BHK interior cost in Noida?

Prioritise the kitchen and wardrobes you use daily, choose laminate or membrane finishes where it won't be noticed, keep false ceilings to the living and dining, mix custom joinery with good ready-made furniture, and lock your full scope before work starts. A single itemised quote also keeps the final figure honest.

Budget to a Plan, Not a Guess

The honest summary: budget around ₹9–15 lakh for a well-finished mid-range 2 BHK in Noida in 2026, put the bulk of it into the kitchen and the two wardrobes, lean on full-height storage and smart space planning, and decide your full scope before any work begins. Done that way, a compact flat ends up feeling far more considered — and more spacious — than the square footage suggests.
