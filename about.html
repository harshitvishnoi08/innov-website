Who We Are

Innov Architects
& Interiors

Innov Architects & Interiors was founded on a conviction that premium architecture must do more than look extraordinary — it must perform commercially, stand the test of time, and carry the client's vision with absolute fidelity. We specialise in resort architecture, luxury hospitality design, banquet complexes, neo-classical estates, and high-value residential projects above ₹5 Crore.

Our practice is structured differently from the typical design studio. Every project — from the initial site analysis to the final handover — is personally overseen by our principal architects and senior designers. We do not delegate the critical thinking. We believe the client at this level deserves the firm's best minds, not its juniors.

Registered with the Council of Architecture, India, we have delivered 500+ projects across Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, and other states. Our portfolio spans luxury resorts, banquet halls, neo-classical private estates, and wellness destinations — each one a long-term asset for its owner.
