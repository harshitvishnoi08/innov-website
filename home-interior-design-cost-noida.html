Home Interior Design Cost in Noida
Per Sq Ft & Full Breakup (2026)

Ar. Udit Vishnoi 13 June 2026 12 min read

"How much will it cost to do up the whole home?" is the first question almost every homeowner asks — and the hardest to answer in one line, because a home interior is really a dozen smaller projects rolled into one. This is a clear, honest breakdown of what home interior design actually costs in Noida in 2026: the per-square-foot rates, the total budget for each flat size, and exactly where the money goes.

Home interior cost in Noida comes down to three things: the size of your home, the quality of materials and finishes you choose, and how much custom work (kitchen, wardrobes, panelling, ceilings) is involved. The figures below are realistic 2026 market rates for Noida and Greater Noida flats — use them to set a budget, then get a proper estimate for your actual space.

Home Interior Cost in Noida — Per Sq Ft (2026)

For full home interiors, the cost is usually quoted per square foot of carpet area, which lets you scale the budget to any flat size. Here is how it breaks down by the quality of the package:

Package Cost / Sq Ft Total (1,200 sq ft)
Basic₹1,000–1,400₹12–17 lakh
Mid-range₹1,500–2,000₹18–24 lakh
Premium / Luxury₹2,500–3,500+₹30–42 lakh+

A basic package gives you a functional, well-finished home — laminate kitchen and wardrobes, selective false ceilings, standard paint and good-value furniture. A mid-range package, where most Noida families land, adds custom joinery with better hardware, more panelling and lighting, and semi-custom furniture. A luxury package brings imported finishes, full false ceilings, statement lighting and bespoke furniture throughout. Bear in mind these rates usually exclude major civil work and flooring — always confirm what is in the scope.

What You Get at Each Budget Level

The same 3 BHK can be finished for ₹12 lakh or ₹30 lakh — the difference is entirely in the materials and the amount of custom work. Here is what separates the three tiers:

Basic (₹1,000–1,400 / sq ft)

Laminate-finish modular kitchen and wardrobes, false ceilings limited to the living and dining, standard emulsion paint, vitrified-tile flooring retained, and a mix of value furniture. A genuinely liveable, clean home — ideal for rental units or a first home.

Mid-range (₹1,500–2,000 / sq ft)

Custom joinery with soft-close hardware, membrane or acrylic kitchen shutters, granite or quartz counters, more panelling and cove lighting, textured paint or wallpaper on feature walls, and semi-custom furniture. This is the sweet spot for most Noida families — a polished, durable home without luxury price tags.

Premium / Luxury (₹2,500–3,500+ / sq ft)

Imported and PU/acrylic finishes, full false ceilings with designer lighting, veneer and stone cladding, statement chandeliers, home automation, and fully bespoke furniture. Every surface is considered. This is where interior design becomes architecture in miniature.

What Drives Home Interior Cost Up or Down

Two identical flats can be done up for very different sums. These are the levers that decide where you land:

  • Amount of custom joinery — every metre of fitted wardrobe, kitchen and panelling is the single biggest cost driver. The more built-in, the higher the bill.
  • Finish quality — laminate vs membrane vs acrylic vs PU on the same cabinet can differ two or three times over.
  • False ceiling coverage — ceiling everywhere, with cove and profile lighting, adds up fast; restricting it to key rooms saves a lot.
  • Furniture: custom vs ready-made — fully bespoke furniture costs far more than mixing in good off-the-shelf pieces.
  • Civil & flooring work — if you are changing flooring or moving walls and plumbing, that is a separate, significant cost on top.
  • Imported materials & automation — imported veneers, stone, lighting and smart-home systems are what push a home into the luxury bracket.

How to Get the Most From Your Budget

Good interior design is as much about spending wisely as spending less. The proven ways to stretch a Noida interior budget:

  • Lock your full scope before you start — mid-project additions are where budgets quietly double.
  • Spend on what you touch daily — the kitchen and wardrobes earn the best return; economise on guest rooms and rarely-used corners.
  • Mix finishes intelligently — acrylic on the kitchen face, laminate inside cabinets; nobody sees the difference, your wallet does.
  • Limit false ceilings to living, dining and the master — flat ceilings with good lighting work perfectly elsewhere.
  • Blend custom and ready-made furniture — built-ins where you need fit, store-bought sofas and chairs where you don't.
  • Get one itemised quote — a single transparent BOQ beats a low headline number hiding extras.

Above all, the spend pays off most when the whole home is designed together — layout, storage, lighting and materials thought through as one scheme rather than bought room by room. That coherence is exactly what a good interior design studio in Noida brings to the table, and it is what turns a budget into a home that looks far more expensive than it cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the home interior design cost in Noida in 2026?

Full home interiors in Noida cost roughly ₹1,000–1,400 per sq ft for a basic fit-out, ₹1,500–2,000 per sq ft for a mid-range home, and ₹2,500–3,500+ per sq ft for premium. For a typical 1,000–1,500 sq ft flat that is about ₹8–25 lakh depending on the scope and finishes.

How much does it cost to do interiors for a 2 BHK or 3 BHK in Noida?

A 2 BHK typically costs ₹6–15 lakh for full interiors and a 3 BHK ₹10–25 lakh, depending on whether you choose a basic, mid-range or luxury package. Budget homes use laminate finishes and ready furniture; luxury homes add custom joinery, full false ceilings, statement lighting and richer materials.

What does home interior cost include?

A full package usually covers the modular kitchen, wardrobes and storage, false ceilings, lighting and electrical points, painting, TV and entertainment units, loose and fixed furniture, and soft furnishings. Civil work, flooring and bathroom renovation are sometimes quoted separately, so always confirm what is in scope.

Why is the kitchen the most expensive part of home interiors?

The modular kitchen and wardrobes are the costliest items because they are custom-built joinery with hardware, and the kitchen also involves countertops, appliances and plumbing. Together they often account for 35–45% of a full home interior budget in Noida.

How can I reduce my home interior cost in Noida?

Decide your full scope upfront, prioritise the kitchen and wardrobes you use daily, choose laminate or membrane finishes where it won't be noticed, keep false ceilings to key rooms, mix custom joinery with good ready-made furniture, and get a single itemised quote. A clear design also prevents the costly mid-project changes that inflate the final bill.

Budget to a Plan, Not a Guess

The honest summary: budget around ₹1,500–2,000 per sq ft for a well-finished mid-range home in Noida in 2026 — roughly ₹9–15 lakh for a 2 BHK and ₹14–22 lakh for a 3 BHK — put the bulk of it into the kitchen and wardrobes, and decide your full scope before any work begins. Done that way, the money buys a home that feels considered in every room rather than patched together one purchase at a time.
