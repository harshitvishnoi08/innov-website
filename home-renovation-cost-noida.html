Home Renovation Cost in Noida
Per Sq Ft & Work-Wise Breakup (2026)

Ar. Udit Vishnoi 8 June 2026 12 min read

Renovating an older flat or house is often the smartest money in real estate — you transform a tired home for a fraction of what a new one costs. But "how much will it cost?" is genuinely hard to answer, because renovation covers everything from a coat of paint to gutting the place to the brick. Here is a clear breakdown of what home renovation actually costs in Noida in 2026, work by work.

Renovation cost depends on three things: how much you change (cosmetic refresh vs full gut job), the quality of materials, and the size of your home. The figures below are realistic 2026 market rates for Noida and Greater Noida flats and independent homes — use them to scope your budget, then get a proper estimate for your space.

For a complete renovation — painting, flooring, false ceilings, electrical, kitchen and bathrooms together — cost is quoted per square foot of carpet area. Here is how it breaks down by the depth of the renovation:

Basic refresh₹800–1,200₹10–14 lakh

As a quick rule of thumb, a typical 1,000–1,500 sq ft flat in Noida costs ₹15–30 lakh for a full renovation. A 2 BHK runs roughly ₹8–18 lakh and a 3 BHK ₹15–30 lakh, depending on whether you are refreshing finishes or gutting and rebuilding. The kitchen and bathrooms are almost always the most expensive parts — exactly where the plumbing and waterproofing live.

Renovate or Rebuild? The 70% Rule

For an independent house, the big question is whether to renovate or tear down and build new. There is a simple, well-tested guideline:

"If your renovation quote crosses about 70% of the cost of building new, stop renovating and rebuild. A quote that high usually means the foundation, plumbing or wiring is failing — and you'll only be papering over it."

Renovation is typically 30–50% cheaper than new construction when the structure, layout and location still work for you. But once you are replacing the bones of the building, the economics flip. What Drives Renovation Cost Up or Down

Two flats the same size can renovate for wildly different sums. These are the factors that decide where you land:

  • Scope — a cosmetic refresh (paint, minor fixes) versus a full gut job (new kitchen, baths, flooring, electrical) is the biggest single variable.
  • Moving plumbing & walls — relocating a kitchen or bathroom, or knocking down walls, adds significant civil and waterproofing cost.
  • Material quality — the same work in laminate vs imported finishes can differ two or three times over.
  • Labour — now 60% or more of a renovation budget; skilled labour in Noida runs ₹300–500 a day per worker.
  • Condition of the existing home — old wiring, leaking plumbing or damp walls add hidden cost once work starts.
  • Furniture & furnishings — often 10–20% of the total, and easy to underestimate.

How to Save on Your Renovation

Renovation is where a clear plan saves the most money, because surprises mid-project are expensive. The proven ways to keep costs down:

  • Define the scope tightly before you start — and resist adding to it once work begins.
  • Keep kitchen and bathroom plumbing where it is — moving wet areas is one of the costliest changes you can make.
  • Retain and refinish what's still sound — refinishing good flooring or recolouring solid cabinets beats ripping them out.
  • Standardise materials — fewer tile and paint types means better rates and less wastage.
  • Renovate in the off-season — January to March, when contractors are less busy and more flexible on price.
  • Keep a 10–15% contingency — old homes always hide a surprise or two; budget for it.

Above all, a renovation pays off most when it is properly designed, not just executed room by room. A coherent plan — layout, lighting, materials and storage thought through together — is what turns a patch-up into a genuine transformation. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the home renovation cost in Noida in 2026?

Full home renovation in Noida costs roughly ₹800–1,200 per sq ft for a basic refresh, ₹1,500–2,000 for a standard renovation, and ₹2,500–3,500+ per sq ft for premium. For a typical 1,000–1,500 sq ft flat that is about ₹15–30 lakh depending on scope and finish.

How much does it cost to renovate a 2 BHK or 3 BHK flat in Noida?

A 2 BHK renovation typically costs ₹8–18 lakh and a 3 BHK ₹15–30 lakh, depending on whether you are refreshing finishes or doing a full gut renovation with new kitchen, bathrooms, flooring, electrical and false ceilings. The kitchen and bathrooms are usually the most expensive parts.

Is it cheaper to renovate or build a new house?

Renovation is usually 30–50% cheaper than new construction when the existing structure, layout and location still meet your needs. As a rule of thumb, if a renovation quote exceeds about 70% of the cost of building new, it usually points to deeper problems with the foundation, plumbing or wiring, and a rebuild becomes the better decision.

How long does a home renovation take in Noida?

A cosmetic refresh takes about 2 to 4 weeks, while a full gut renovation of a 2–3 BHK typically runs 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope and material lead times. Civil and waterproofing work, plus society or RWA approvals, are the main factors that affect the schedule.

How can I reduce my home renovation cost in Noida?

Define the scope tightly before starting, keep kitchen and bathroom plumbing in their existing positions, retain and refinish what is still sound, standardise materials, and get multiple itemised quotes. Scheduling in the off-season and keeping a 10–15% contingency also help control the final figure.

What is the home renovation rate per sq ft in Noida?

Home renovation rates in Noida run roughly ₹800–1,200 per sq ft for a basic refresh, ₹1,500–2,000 per sq ft for a standard renovation, and ₹2,500–3,500+ per sq ft for a premium finish. Kitchen and bathroom work carries the highest per-sq-ft rate since it involves plumbing, waterproofing and fittings, not just flooring and paint.

How much does flat renovation cost in Noida?

Flat renovation cost in Noida depends mainly on size and scope: a 2 BHK typically runs ₹8–18 lakh and a 3 BHK ₹15–30 lakh. A cosmetic flat renovation (paint, flooring, false ceiling) sits at the lower end, while a full gut renovation with new kitchen, bathrooms and electrical rewiring pushes toward the upper end.

Renovate to a Plan, Not on the Fly

The honest summary: budget around ₹1,500–2,000 per sq ft for a good standard renovation in Noida in 2026 — roughly ₹15–25 lakh for an average flat — put the bulk of it into the kitchen and bathrooms, lock your scope before work starts, and keep a contingency for what old homes inevitably hide. Done well, a renovation gives you a brand-new home at a fraction of the cost of buying one.
