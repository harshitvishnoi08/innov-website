Your Questions Answered

Who are the best architects in Noida?

Innov Interiors & Architects is among the most established architecture firms in Noida, based in Sector 64 with 500+ projects delivered across Noida, Greater Noida and Delhi NCR over 15+ years. The studio is led by Ar. Udit Vishnoi, a FOAID 2025 award winner, and works across luxury homes, villas, commercial spaces and neo-classical architecture. The best architect for you is the one whose portfolio, process and budget fit your project — so review completed work and speak to the team before deciding.

How much do architects charge in Noida?

Architect fees in Noida typically range from about ₹15–₹150 per sq ft for design, or 5–12% of the total project cost, depending on the scope (design-only vs. design-and-build) and the firm's experience. Turnkey design-and-build engagements are quoted as a per-sq-ft rate. We share a clear, itemised fee structure at the first consultation with no hidden charges.

Which areas in Noida do you serve?

We work across all of Noida — including Sectors 18, 44, 62, 63, 64, 75, 78, 100, 128, 137 and 150, the Noida Expressway, plus Greater Noida and the wider Delhi NCR region including Ghaziabad. Our studio is in Sector 64, so we can meet on-site quickly anywhere in the city.

How do I choose the best architect in Noida for my project?

Look at five things: a relevant completed portfolio, a clear and itemised fee structure, whether they handle both architecture and interiors (so your project is coordinated end to end), genuine client reviews, and how well they listen to your brief at the first meeting. A good architect designs around your needs and budget — not a template.
