See the sessions

Saturday sessions

Every Saturday someone from outside the studio comes in to teach us something. Suppliers of wood and other materials bring samples, show how their products are made and used, and answer the team's questions. It's how our designers learn far more than any single project would teach them.

Celebrations

When the festive season arrives, the drawings pause and the whole studio celebrates together.

Visits
